# Fernanda Melo

**Public policy researcher and consultant** working across migration, gender, housing, social inclusion, and sustainable development in the Global South. I combine qualitative and mixed-methods research with evaluation and policy analysis to connect rigorous inquiry with practical recommendations.

[ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-3672-6036) · [Master's dissertation](https://teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/100/100138/tde-19082026-102822/pt-br.html)

## Research and policy work

### Evaluation of FINEP Mulheres Inovadoras | UNESCO PoliCyTLab

Contributed to an evaluation of Brazil's FINEP programme for women innovators (October 2025–June 2026). Conducted and analyzed more than 30 interviews, triangulated evidence, mapped stakeholders and the theory of change, and developed 25 improvement proposals with indicators.

### Master's research | University of São Paulo

An ethnographic case study with migrant women in the Jean-Jacques Dessalines occupation. The research brings together fieldwork and interviews, analysis of institutional documents, and mapping of municipal migration policy. Supported by FAPESP.

### Undergraduate research | CNPq

Studied political mobilization and resistance in Palestine using qualitative and documentary methods, including systematic source cataloguing. The project received an honorable mention and nomination.

### Monitoring, evaluation and learning | Mandê

Built a monitoring and evaluation system, validated indicators, and used mixed methods across several concurrent projects. Produced reporting and data visualizations to support programme decisions.

### Policy research and consulting | Hu(main)e

Cofounded a consultancy focused on sustainable development, gender equity, and social inclusion, contributing research and policy analysis for public-interest work.
